Verification cases
==================

The verification cases quantify Kynema-SGF numerical behavior against
analytical solutions. They provide focused, reproducible studies rather than
application-scale benchmarks.

* `Laminar channel flow <verification/laminar_channel_flow/README.html>`__:
  pressure-driven Poiseuille flow in flat and 45-degree immersed-boundary
  channels, with a no-immersed-boundary reference study.

  * `Setup and execution <verification/laminar_channel_flow/README.html#generate-solver-inputs>`__
  * `Convergence analysis <verification/laminar_channel_flow/README.html#analyze-convergence>`__
  * `Results <verification/laminar_channel_flow/README.html#outputs>`__
